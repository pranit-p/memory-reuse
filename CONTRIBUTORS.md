## How to get listed

1. Open a pull request with your change (code, docs, tests, or a fix).
2. In the same PR, add yourself to the **Contributors** section above.
3. Once the PR is reviewed and merged, you're officially credited here and on
   the GitHub Contributors graph.

Every merged contribution counts — from a one-line typo fix to a major feature.
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for setup and guidelines.
